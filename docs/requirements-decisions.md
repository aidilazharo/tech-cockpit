## 3. SAD (System Architecture Design)

- One **living SAD per business application**.
- Sections: drafted from research, then adapted by the team.
- Every change creates a new **version** with history and diff; a version becomes current only after the **configurable approval workflow** approves it.
- Editors: application owner team, solution architects, enterprise architects, vendors assigned to that application.
- SAD **security sections are restricted** data.

## 5. Solution architecture (inside a draft business case)

- SA builds the solution diagram in **React Flow**, or **imports draw.io** files into the editable diagram. Visio and images stay as attachments.
- **Network zones are free per diagram** (SA creates them).
- SA links TRM applications with impact type: **new, modified, integrated with, decommissioned**.
- Each connection carries: protocol + port, direction + pattern (API/file/event/DB), data classification, authentication + encryption.
- On approval, the solution creates **draft TRM/SAD updates** that application owners or EA confirm.

### ARB flow

- **Fixed ARB group** maintained by admins; every solution goes to all members in parallel.
- **All must approve.** A rejecting reviewer's comments go back to the SA; after the SA replies, **only the rejecting reviewers** review again.
- **ARB chair** gives final approval after all reviewers approve.
- The **enterprise architect is the ARB chair and also an ARB reviewer** (decided 2026-10-22). The roles stay separate (`enterprise-architect`, `arb-member`, `arb-chair`) and are all assigned to that person, so they approve twice: once with the other reviewers, then the final chair approval. If the jobs are split later, only the role assignments change.
- ARB runs **in parallel** with the business case chain; the **last business case approval is blocked** until the ARB chair approves.

## 6. Approval engine

- **Admin-configurable in a maintenance UI** (no release needed): amount bands and boundaries, approvers per step, steps and order (sequential/parallel), conditions on other fields (department, portfolio, cost category, etc.).
- Unavailable approver: **out-of-office delegate**, **auto-escalate after per-step SLA**, **admin reassign** (reason logged), **group steps** (first member to act completes it).
- Reject → back to requestor → resubmission **resumes at the rejecting step** (earlier approvals kept).
- Separate **"Return for more info"** action (not counted as rejection).
- Notifications: **in-app inbox** now; Microsoft Teams and email later.
- Used by: business case, ARB, SAD versions, project closure (and future modules via the skill file).
- Approval Centre shows every item's current step and who it's waiting on.

### Approval Centre, notifications and maintenance (decided 2026-10-21)

- **Approval Centre views**: Waiting for me, My requests, All approvals (people with approval-tracking permission, within their scope; admin reassign), Done by me. Each item shows its current step and who it is waiting on; out-of-office delegation is managed there.
- **In-app notifications** for: a task waiting for me (new, re-review, reassigned, escalated, or as a delegate); the outcome of my requests (approved, returned with reason, cancelled); questions and answers (approver asks the requester, requester answers); and an **SLA reminder when 75% of a step's SLA has passed**. Kept **90 days** (the audit log is the permanent record). Teams and email later.
- **Approval-rule maintenance screen** with **fully nested condition groups** (all/any, up to 5 levels), steps, approvers, quorum, SLA, escalation, re-review and gates; drafts are published as new versions.
- **Qi date picker** built now (role assignment start/end dates first; reused by PMO and delegation).
- **Audit log viewer**: filter by person, action, resource and date range; entry details with before/after changes. No export for now.
- Admin gaps: **rename and deactivate departments** (code fixed); admins with "revoke any API key" **see and revoke a user's keys** on the user page.

## 7. RBAC and data protection

- Roles and scopes are **assigned in the app** (Okta provides identity only).
- **Financial data visibility is configurable per role.** Fixed rules on top:
  - Portfolio lead: own portfolio stream only.
  - Product manager: own submitted financial data only.
  - Approver: from when their step becomes active, and keeps read access after acting.
  - Vendor: **never**.
- Other restricted data: **contract terms, staff personal data**. SAD sections: per-section View/Edit permissions (vendors included, decided 2026-10-22).
- Vendors sign in through the **same Okta org**. Vendor access: SADs of assigned apps, TRM entries of assigned apps (read), vendor portal, documents of assigned projects.
- **Configurable RBAC (decided 2026-10-22, built 2026-10-23):** of the 27 access rules fixed in code, 14 become configurable (vendor restrictions decided by roles, 6 new permissions, a "Can act for staff" role setting, approvers-see-financials per approval type, an admin-maintained field classification) and 13 stay fixed (system administrator, anti-escalation, separation of duties, nobody disables their own account: 2026-10-23). Detail: [modules/rbac-configurable.md](modules/rbac-configurable.md).
- **Audit log records all changes and all views.**
- **System administrator sees all classified data (decided 2026-10-28, Terry).** The seeded System administrator role now carries data access `all` for financial, contract and personal data (it was `none`, so admins saw "Restricted" on TRM costs, people's names and SAD names). It is one migration (`Migration20261028065500_system_admin_data_access`); the role stays locked (rules 10 and 11), vendors can still never hold it, and every other seeded role is unchanged. Admin actions are still audited, views included.
- Compliance: **BNM RMiT, PDPA (Malaysia), ISO 27001, OWASP ASVS Level 2.**

### Administration screens (decided 2026-10-21)

- **Left side navigation** for the whole app (grouped: Home, modules, Administration). Collapses to icons on tablet, slide-out drawer on phone. Items appear only when the user holds the permission. Qi has no navigation component; it is designed from Qi tokens.
- Screens: Users (search, add, enable/disable, department, role assignments), Roles (permissions and data access), Departments, and **My API keys** (from the account menu).
- Role assignment scopes offered now: **Global and Department**. Portfolio, stream, application and vendor scopes appear when their modules add that data.

## 10. Authentication and API

- **Okta SAML only**, using the **Okta dev tenant** for local and test logins (no mock IdP). Login-dependent work waits for tenant details.
- After SAML login: **short-lived JWT access token + refresh token** in httpOnly, Secure cookies.
- Session timings (decided 2026-10-21), **configurable through environment settings**: access token **15 minutes** (refreshed silently), idle timeout **30 minutes**, absolute limit **240 minutes**.
- **User management is in TechCockpit; Okta is for sign-in only** (decided 2026-10-23, replaces JIT accounts and the Okta vendor flag): only people added on the Users page can sign in, identified by their **Okta username** (SAML NameID, ignoring case); others are refused and listed under "Refused sign-ins" (kept 90 days). Admins set username, email, names and **Employee/Vendor** in the app; Okta only refreshes the email. Signed-in users without a role in force see a full "no access" page. Detail: [modules/user-management.md](modules/user-management.md).
- A vendor never acts as system administrator: the role is never given to a vendor, and a user cannot be changed to Vendor while holding it.
- Logout: **TechCockpit session only** (no Okta single logout).
- Non-browser callers: personal API keys bound to the user's RBAC.
- API-first, OpenAPI 3.x. **Swagger UI in all environments, behind login** and an API-docs permission.

How it is built (2026-10-21, `NestJS_Backend/src/modules/auth`):

- **SP-initiated** SAML 2.0 with `@node-saml/node-saml` 5.1 (HTTP-Redirect AuthnRequest, HTTP-POST response). Okta metadata is fetched from `SAML_IDP_METADATA_URL` and refreshed daily, so Okta certificate rotation needs no redeploy; the last good copy is kept if Okta is unreachable.
- Checks on every response: response **and** assertion signatures, issuer, audience, timestamps (60 s clock skew), Destination, Recipient, InResponseTo against an outstanding request (PostgreSQL, 10 min), **one-time use** (replay), and a **browser-binding cookie** so a response is only accepted by the browser that started the sign-in (login CSRF). node-saml 5.1 does not check the issuer, Destination or Recipient of sign-in responses; TechCockpit does.
- IdP-initiated sign-in (Okta dashboard tile) is restarted as SP-initiated. `returnTo` is stored server-side with the request (RelayState is not trusted) and must be an in-app path.
- Cookies: `tc_access` (JWT HS256, `SESSION_JWT_SECRET`, SameSite=Lax, path `/api`), `tc_refresh` (opaque, hash stored, **rotated on every refresh**, SameSite=Strict, path `/api/v1/auth`). All httpOnly and Secure (browsers accept Secure on `http://localhost`). Every request re-checks the session row, so sign-out and disabling a user take effect at once.
- Refresh-token **reuse after a 30-second grace period revokes the session** (theft detection).
- **CSRF**: cookie-authenticated state-changing requests must carry an `Origin` from `FRONTEND_ORIGINS` or `API_BASE_URL`; API keys are unaffected.
- Audit: `auth.login`, `auth.login.failed` (with reason), `auth.logout`, `auth.refresh.failed`, JIT `user.created` / Okta-driven `user.updated`; every audited request records its `session_id`.
- Housekeeping (hourly): ended sessions are deleted after **30 days**, stale SAML requests after a day. The audit log keeps sign-in history permanently.
- First administrator: `npm run bootstrap:admin -- --username <Okta username> --email <email> ...` (only added people can sign in).
- Frontend: **React Router 8** (data mode, decided 2026-10-21). `/design-system` is the public component gallery; `/signin?returnTo=&error=` is the sign-in page; every other page sits under a protected layout that redirects to `/signin` (keeping the page as `returnTo`) when there is no session. The API client renews the access token before expiry and on a 401, and returns to the sign-in page when the session ends.
- Header: **avatar account menu** (decided 2026-10-21) with name, email, Vendor tag, theme (System/Light/Dark) and Sign out. Qi has no menu component, so `Menu` follows Qi dropdown styling on Base UI Menu; `Avatar` follows Qi avatar guidance (initials until an image exists).
